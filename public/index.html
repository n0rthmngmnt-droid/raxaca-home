Questions

Before you buy

What do I get after paying?

A download link by email, usually within a minute. The PDF opens on any phone, tablet or computer and prints cleanly — the checklists and shopping lists are laid out for A4 and US Letter.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every guide is a one-time payment. You keep the file for good, and you receive free updates whenever that edition is revised.

Metric or US measurements?

Both. Every recipe lists grams and millilitres alongside cups and ounces, and oven temperatures in °C and °F.

Which guide should I start with?

If dinner is the problem, The Weeknight Kitchen. If you want one satisfying weekend project, The Sourdough Handbook. If the house itself is the problem, The Home Reset.

What will appear on my card statement?

Charges show as RAXACA, billed by RAXACA LLC, the company behind Raxaca Home. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. Questions about a charge: contact us before contacting your bank and we'll sort it out quickly.

Can I get a refund?

Digital files can't be returned, but if a guide isn't what you expected, email info@raxaca.com within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund it in full. Details in our refund policy.
