About the company

Raxaca Home is the public business name of RAXACA LLC, a limited liability company organised in Wyoming, United States, with its business address at 30 N Gould St, Ste 26295, Sheridan, WY 82801. We publish digital guides for cooking, baking and home organisation, sold as PDF downloads.

Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout page and never reach our servers. Purchases appear on your statement as RAXACA. If you don't recognise a charge, contact us first — we can look it up by the email used at checkout and resolve it faster than a chargeback.

Delivery

Every purchase is delivered by email as a download link, normally within a minute of payment. If it hasn't arrived after ten minutes, check your spam folder, then email us with the address you used at checkout and we'll resend it.
